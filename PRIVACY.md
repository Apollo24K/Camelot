# Camelot Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect account information across our services.

## Information We Collect

1. **Discord user IDs:** We use Discord user IDs to distinguish one account from another. This is the identifying value required to associate a Discord user with a Camelot account.
2. **Account details:** We store the progress and other account details required to provide our services. This information is linked to the user's Discord account.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We store only the user information needed for our services to work properly. Users may request the complete deletion of their accumulated data through the [Camelot Support server](https://discord.gg/myy9PBCdEW).

## Data Sharing and Access

We do not share collected account or statistical data with anyone outside the Camelot staff. The bot owner is the only person with direct access to the database.

## Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if our data practices change and will notify users of any changes.
